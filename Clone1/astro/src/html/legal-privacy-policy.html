Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Prismaclad (“we”, “us”) collects through prismaclad.com, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. The site is a marketing and research site. It has no accounts, no checkout, and no advertising.

1. What we collect

Information you send us. The contact form asks for your name, email address and a message. Phone number and company are optional. If you email contact@prismaclad.com directly, we receive your address and whatever you write. Please don't include anything sensitive that we don't need to scope a facade project.

Information collected automatically. When you visit, our hosting provider and our analytics service record standard request data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and approximate location derived from the IP address.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask for one, prepare a proposal
  • To understand which pages and research posts are read, so we can improve them
  • To keep the site secure and working
  • To meet legal obligations

We don't sell your information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't build profiles for resale.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Ahrefs Analytics to measure site traffic. We do not run advertising, retargeting, or social media tracking pixels. Fonts and other assets are served from our own domain. If we add any tool that sets non-essential cookies, we will list it here first.

4. Who handles your information

We share information only with the providers that run the site:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and delivers contact form submissions to our inbox by email
  • Ahrefs provides site analytics

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect our rights, and to a successor if Prismaclad is sold or merges.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept for as long as needed to respond and to keep a reasonable record of our business relationship, then deleted. Analytics data is kept in aggregate for as long as it is useful for understanding traffic. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California, the EU, the UK and several other jurisdictions have these rights by law. To use any of them, email contact@prismaclad.com. We will respond within the time the law allows and may need to verify your identity first. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are sent to us over an encrypted connection. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it.

8. International transfers

Our providers operate globally, so information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

This site is written for business audiences and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other sites

Pages on this site link to outside sources, news coverage and our social profiles. We don't control those sites and this policy doesn't cover them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on the page for a reasonable period.
